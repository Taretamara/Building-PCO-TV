# **PCO TV**

## **Product Requirements Document**

**Product:** PCO TV  
**Product Type:** Television streaming and content platform  
**Primary Audience:** LoveWorld followers worldwide, with accessibility for the broader Christian audience  
**Document Status:** Product Concept / V1 PRD

---

# **1\. Product Vision**

**PCO TV is a dedicated television experience for watching Pastor Chris Oyakhilome's messages, enjoying LoveWorld music, and discovering Christian programming from the comfort of home.**

The product should feel familiar and effortless—users should be able to turn on their TV, find something meaningful to watch or listen to, and begin within seconds.

PCO TV should become a **home destination for LoveWorld content**, rather than simply being another place where videos are available.

### **Product principle**

> **Turn on. Find something meaningful. Watch.**

---

# **2\. Target Users**

### **Primary audience**

**LoveWorld followers**

People who already follow Pastor Chris and LoveWorld and want convenient access to their content on a television.

They may want to:

* Watch Pastor Chris's latest messages  
* Rewatch older teachings  
* Follow specific programs or events  
* Listen to LoveWorld music  
* Watch live programming  
* Save favorite content  
* Continue watching something they started earlier

### **Secondary audience**

**Christians discovering LoveWorld**

Someone who may not already be deeply familiar with LoveWorld should still be able to open PCO TV and understand what is available.

The experience should therefore avoid requiring prior knowledge of LoveWorld programs or terminology.

---

# **3\. Core Product Experience**

PCO TV will have a **simple category-based structure with personalized content embedded throughout the experience.**

## **Primary navigation**

1. **Home**  
2. **Messages**  
3. **Music**  
4. **Live**  
5. **Programs**  
6. **Favorites**

Search should also be easily accessible from every major area.

---

# **4\. Home**

The Home screen is the user's starting point.

Rather than opening directly into a single video, PCO TV should present a simple dashboard that combines navigation with personalized recommendations.

### **Home sections**

**Continue Watching**

Content the user has previously started but not finished.

**Recommended for You**

Content selected based on the user's viewing and listening activity.

**Live Now**

A prominent area showing currently available live programming.

**Pastor Chris**

A dedicated area for Pastor Chris's messages and teachings.

**LoveWorld Music**

A dedicated area for music.

**Recently Added**

Newly available content.

**Popular**

Content that is receiving significant attention from PCO TV users.

### **Home-screen principle**

The home screen should not feel crowded.

The user should immediately understand:

> **What can I watch right now?**

---

# **5\. Messages**

Messages are one of the core experiences of PCO TV.

Users should have multiple ways to discover content.

## **Search**

Users can search for:

* Message titles  
* Topics  
* Programs  
* Events  
* Speakers  
* Artists  
* Keywords

Example:

> "faith"

could surface messages, programs, and related content associated with faith.

## **Browse by Topic**

Examples:

* Faith  
* Healing  
* Prayer  
* Holy Spirit  
* Christian living  
* Spiritual growth  
* Family  
* Leadership  
* Purpose  
* Evangelism

Topics should make older content easier to discover.

## **Browse by Program/Event**

Users can browse content associated with specific programs and events.

Examples may include:

* Healing Streams  
* Global Communion  
* Your Loveworld  
* Pastor Chris Live  
* Special programs  
* Conferences and events

## **Browse by Date**

Users can view recent content and explore older content chronologically.

---

# **6\. Message Details**

Selecting a message opens its dedicated viewing page.

The page should display:

* Title  
* Pastor/speaker  
* Program or event  
* Topic  
* Description  
* Duration  
* Favorite  
* Save for Later  
* Share  
* Related content

### **Watch Next**

When a message finishes, PCO TV should automatically recommend another relevant piece of content.

The recommendation should prioritize:

1. Another message in the same series/program  
2. Another message on the same topic  
3. Content related to the user's viewing history  
4. Recently added relevant content

The goal is to make discovering the next message effortless.

---

# **7\. Continue Watching**

If a user leaves a message before finishing it, PCO TV should remember their progress.

When they return:

> **Continue Watching**

should allow them to immediately resume.

This should work across the user's PCO TV experience so that the user doesn't have to remember where they stopped.

---

# **8\. Favorites**

Users should be able to save content they particularly enjoy.

Favorites should support:

* Messages  
* Music  
* Programs  
* Other supported content

The Favorites area should be intentionally simple.

Suggested organization:

**All | Messages | Music | Programs**

---

# **9\. Save for Later**

Favorites and Save for Later should serve different purposes.

### **Favorites**

Content the user considers personally important or wants easy long-term access to.

### **Save for Later**

Content the user intends to watch/listen to later.

This prevents the Favorites section from becoming a giant list of everything the user has ever saved.

---

# **10\. Music**

Music should be a first-class part of PCO TV rather than an afterthought.

## **Music home**

Users should see:

* Featured music  
* Latest releases  
* Popular songs  
* Artists  
* Albums  
* Playlists  
* Favorites  
* Recently played

## **Artists**

Users should be able to browse LoveWorld artists and singers.

Selecting an artist should show:

* Songs  
* Albums  
* Playlists  
* Related artists/content

## **Albums**

Each album should provide:

* Album artwork  
* Track list  
* Play  
* Add to Favorites  
* Save  
* Play All

---

# **11\. Playlists**

Playlists would be particularly valuable for television listening.

PCO TV should provide curated playlists such as:

* LoveWorld Worship  
* Praise  
* Christian Inspiration  
* Morning Worship  
* Evening Worship  
* New Releases  
* Pastor Chris Recommended  
* Popular LoveWorld Songs

Users should also eventually be able to create their own playlists.

### **Recommendation**

Make **curated playlists available from the beginning**, but treat user-created playlists as a later feature if keeping the first release focused.

---

# **12\. Music Listening Experience**

When music is playing, the user should not need to remain on the music page.

The experience should allow:

* Play/Pause  
* Previous/Next  
* Favorite  
* Add to Playlist  
* View Album  
* View Artist  
* Continue Playing

The TV experience should also support **background-style listening**, where appropriate, so users can listen to music without needing to continuously interact with the screen.

---

# **13\. Live**

Live should be one of the most prominent areas of PCO TV.

The Live section should show:

### **Live Now**

What is currently broadcasting.

### **Upcoming**

Programs scheduled to begin soon.

### **Recently Live**

Recently completed live broadcasts that are available to watch.

When nothing is live, PCO TV should not leave the user at an empty screen.

Instead:

> **Nothing is live right now.**

followed by:

* Upcoming programs  
* Recent broadcasts  
* Recommended messages

---

# **14\. Programs**

Programs provide another way to organize the content library.

Each program gets its own page.

A program page could contain:

* Program description  
* Latest episodes  
* Previous episodes  
* Related messages  
* Upcoming broadcasts  
* Favorite Program

Users should be able to **follow a program**.

Following a program allows PCO TV to surface new episodes on the user's Home screen.

---

# **15\. Notifications**

Notifications should focus on things the user actually cares about.

Examples:

> **Pastor Chris is live now.**

> **A new episode of \[Program\] is available.**

> **New music from \[Artist\] is now available.**

> **Your saved message is now available to watch.**

Users should be able to control which types of notifications they receive.

---

# **16\. Search**

Search should be available throughout PCO TV.

The search experience should return different types of results together.

For example, searching:

> **Healing**

could produce:

### **Messages**

Relevant teachings

### **Programs**

Programs containing relevant content

### **Music**

Relevant songs

### **Events**

Relevant events

### **Artists**

Relevant artists where appropriate

This makes PCO TV feel like **one unified content library**, rather than several disconnected sections.

---

# **17\. Discovery**

PCO TV should actively help users discover content without making the experience feel overly algorithmic.

Recommended sections can include:

### **Because You Watched**

Related content based on viewing history.

### **More From This Program**

Other content from the same program.

### **More Like This**

Content with similar topics.

### **You May Also Like**

Broader recommendations.

### **New on PCO TV**

Recently added content.

### **Popular on PCO TV**

Content currently receiving attention.

---

# **18\. User Accounts**

A user account should allow PCO TV to remember their experience.

Account features should include:

* Continue Watching  
* Favorites  
* Saved content  
* Playlists  
* Followed programs  
* Viewing/listening history  
* Personalized recommendations

### **Recommendation**

Make account creation **optional at first use**.

A user should be able to explore and watch available content without feeling forced into registration immediately.

Then introduce account benefits naturally:

> **Sign in to save your favorites and continue watching across your devices.**

This reduces friction when someone first opens the product.

---

# **19\. Family Experience**

Because PCO TV is designed for the television, it should work well for households.

The experience should be:

* Simple  
* Easy to navigate  
* Large and readable  
* Comfortable to use from a distance  
* Appropriate for shared viewing

### **Family profiles**

I recommend eventually supporting profiles.

For example:

**Tare**  
**Mom**  
**Dad**  
**Kids**

Each profile could have its own:

* Favorites  
* Continue Watching  
* Recommendations  
* Playlists

* Kids

* Family programming

# **21\. Sharing**

Users should be able to share a message, song, program, or event with someone else.

Sharing should communicate:

* What the content is  
* Who it's from  
* Where to watch/listen

The recipient should be able to open the content directly.

# **22\. Accessibility**

PCO TV should be designed so that people with different abilities can comfortably use it.

Important product considerations include:

* Clear text  
* Strong visual contrast  
* Simple navigation  
* Captions where available  
* Audio descriptions where available  
* Clear playback controls  
* Straightforward language

Accessibility should be treated as part of the core product experience rather than an optional addition.

---

# **23\. International Audience**

Because the primary audience is global, PCO TV should be designed for international use from the beginning.

The product should eventually support:

* Multiple languages  
* Subtitles  
* Different regional content  
* Localized program information  
* International availability

### **Recommendation**

Start with English, but design the content experience so additional languages can be introduced naturally.

# **35\. Final Product Recommendation**

I would **not** position PCO TV as merely "Pastor Chris's YouTube on a TV."

I would build it as a **LoveWorld media platform centered around Pastor Chris**, with three major pillars:

### **WATCH**

Pastor Chris's messages, programs, events, and Christian programming.

### **LISTEN**

LoveWorld singers, music, albums, and playlists.

### **LIVE**

Live broadcasts and upcoming programming.

Then layer personalization across all three:

> **Continue Watching → Favorites → Recommended → Followed Programs → Watch Next**

# **36\. User Roles & Permissions**

PCO TV should have **three core user roles**.

### **1\. Customer / Viewer**

The primary end user of PCO TV.

Customers can:

* Browse content
* Watch Pastor Chris's messages
* Listen to LoveWorld music
* Watch live programming
* Search for content
* Add content to Favorites
* Save content for later
* Follow programs
* Manage their personal profile
* Receive personalized recommendations
* Resume content they previously started

### **2\. Content Admin**

The administrative role responsible for managing the content available on PCO TV.

Content Admins can:

* Add and manage messages
* Add and manage music, artists, and albums
* Create and manage programs
* Organize content by topics, programs, and events
* Manage content descriptions, titles, and artwork
* Manage featured content
* Manage Live programming
* Control content availability
* Organize and curate the Home page

### **3\. Super Admin**

The highest-level administrative role with full platform access.

Super Admins can:

* Manage customers/users
* Create, manage, and remove admins
* Manage all content
* Manage platform-wide settings
* Manage permissions
* View overall platform activity
* Perform all functions available to Content Admins

### **Role Structure**

Use this hierarchy:

**Customer / Viewer → Content Admin → Super Admin**

Do not add additional roles at this stage. The product should begin with these three roles and allow specialized roles such as Music Manager, Live Manager, Editorial Manager, or Support Admin to be introduced later if the platform grows.

### **Important Product Direction**

Keep the role system simple for the initial version. Do not introduce unnecessary complexity.

The PRD should continue to reflect the existing product direction:

* PCO TV is primarily designed for LoveWorld followers worldwide.
* It is also accessible to Christians who are discovering LoveWorld.
* The primary TV navigation is **Home, Messages, Music, Live, Programs, Favorites, and Search**.
* The Home experience combines a simple category-based structure with personalization such as **Continue Watching, Recommended for You, Live Now, Recently Added, and Popular**.
* Messages support **Search, Topics, Programs/Events, and Recent Messages**.
* Selecting content includes a **Watch Next** experience.
* PCO TV is intended to be a dedicated television destination for Pastor Chris's messages, LoveWorld music, and LoveWorld programming.
