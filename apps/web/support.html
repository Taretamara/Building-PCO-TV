Support

Quick answers

Install the app? Tap Install app in the top bar (computer/Android), or iPhone Share → Add to Home Screen.

Videos don't play real footage? This build uses sample content and preview stand-ins; full streaming arrives with the licensed catalog.

Forgot a profile PIN? PINs can't be recovered by design — nobody, including us, can unlock it. Delete the locked profile (✕ on the profile screen) and create a fresh one; anything saved under it is gone with it, so keep PINs written down somewhere safe.

Subscribe test? Home → ★ Subscribe → test email → Paystack test checkout → card 4084084084084081. No real charge in test mode.

Something offensive in reflections? Tap Report under any comment to hide it on your device. Shared moderation arrives with multi-user community.

Offline? Installed app keeps working with saved content; look for the offline bar. Sync resumes when you're back online.

Contact us

Email: support@pco.tv — include your device (Android/iPhone/computer), what you tapped, and any exact error text.
