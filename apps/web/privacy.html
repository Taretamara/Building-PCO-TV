PCO TV · PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026. PCO TV is currently a demo build; this policy describes what the app does today.

What we collect

Account email. If you create an account, we store your email address to sign you in on any device.

Your activity. Favorites, saved items, followed programs, watch progress, reflections you post, and subscription status — synced so they follow your account.

Device-only data. Profiles, profile PINs (stored as irreversible hashes, never the PIN itself), and app preferences stay in your browser and never leave your device.

Payments. Card and payment details are entered on Paystack's pages and processed by Paystack. We never see or store card numbers — we only receive confirmation that a subscription is active.

What we don't collect

No advertising trackers, no sale of personal data, no background location. Analytics is limited to basic, anonymous usage counts needed to keep the service running.

How data is protected

Accounts are protected by passwords and database rules that let each user touch only their own data. Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). PIN-locked profiles are additionally encrypted on-device; forgetting a PIN makes that profile's local data unrecoverable — even to us.

Children and family

PCO TV is designed for family viewing, including Kids profiles. Accounts must be created by an adult; children's use should be supervised by a parent or guardian.

Your rights

You can sign out anytime (stops syncing). To delete your account and cloud data, email us (below) from your account address and we will remove it. To erase device data, use your browser's site-data controls or delete profiles in-app.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes (for example, when real streaming content or new providers launch). Continued use after changes means acceptance.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: support@pco.tv.
